About

I recently completed my PhD in Molecular and Cell Biology at Université Grenoble Alpes, carrying out my research at CEA Grenoble. My doctoral work investigated the integration of light and CO₂ signals in the green microalga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii.

I enjoy working across disciplines and am particularly interested in how organisms adapt to challenging environments and what these mechanisms can tell us about the limits of life.
