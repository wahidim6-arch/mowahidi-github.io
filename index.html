About

I obtained my PhD in molecular and cell biology from Grenoble Alpes University (Grenoble, France) and I hold two MSc degrees in Developmental and molecular evolutionary biology from Sorbonne University (Paris, France) and Synthetic biology from Paris-Saclay University. I did my bachelor's degree at Ruhr-Universität Bochum (Germany) with emphasis on cancer biology and minors in chemistry and physics.

For my PhD I focused on elucidating molecular regulators involved in photoprotection and the CO2 concentrating mechanism in a green photosynthetic microorganism (Chlamydomonas reinhardtii) and started a project to unravel structural insights into proterties on an improtant master regulator of these mechanisms. The project allowed me to not only expand ony m expertise im molecular biology, but also involved a significant bioinformatics part as a minor strucutral protein biology technical part.

I have diverse scientific interests, which can be noticed in the number of internships I have done internationally in different sub-fields of biology ranging from cancer bilogy, stem cell & developmental biology, synthetic biology, microbiology & plant biology.

Research experience

Doctoral research · 2022–2026
LPCV, CEA Grenoble · Université Grenoble Alpes (France)

Investigated genome-wide regulatory mechanisms underlying light and CO₂ signal integration in Chlamydomonas reinhardtii. I unraveled candidates for transcriptional regulators of photoprotection and the CO₂ concentrating mechanism through building a gene-regulatory network based on RNA-sequenced samples from Chlamydomonas cells in different lab-controlled environmental conditions.

The project involved diverse techniques in molecular biology, algal cellular biology, bioninformatics for RNA-sequencing analysis using R, gene-editing via CRISPR-cas9, biochemistry and structural protein biology-related approaches.

Supervised by Dr. Dimitris Petroutsos (Uppsala University) and Dr. Romain Blanc-Mathieu).

Master's thesis internship in Synthetic Microbiology · 02.2022–06.2022
I2BC · CNRS- Gif-sur-yvette (France)

Worked on synthetically refactoring the biosynthetic gene cluster responsible for the production of the antibiotic pyrollamide congocidine in Actinobacteria in the lab of Dr. Sylvie Lautru.

Master's thesis internship in Marine Molecular Development · 01.2021–06.2021
SARS Centre for Marine Molecular Biology · University of Bergen (Norway)

I investigated the function of 'homeobox-genes' in the development of the epithelium of the primitive chordate Oikopleura dioica in the lab of Dr. Daniel Chourrout.

Summer internship in Plant development · 07.2020–09.2020
Centre for Organismal Studies · University of Heidelberg (Germany)

Contributed briefly to the research of cellular mechanisms controlling later-root development in Arabidopsis thaliana in the lab of Dr. Alexis Maizel.

Internship in Epigenetics, stem-cells and neurodevelopment · 01.2020–06.2020
